# Reflection models

## Learning through Reflection

[Reflective learning](https://courses.lumenlearning.com/healthcarecommunication/chapter/reflective-learning/) is where we put some time aside to think about what we have learned. Reflection deepens learning as it encourages continuous learning. We consider what was learned, how it was learned and how the information will be used in the future.

In this last module take some time to look over your discussion board posts and your self-assessment rubrics  (pre and post). Has anything changed for you?

If you are new to reflecting, look at these  [Reflection models](https://libguides.cam.ac.uk/reflectivepracticetoolkit/models)   . This link provides 4 different types of reflection models. These models will help guide your reflection process so that you can share with the rest of the group where you started, what changed, where you are now and what is next.
