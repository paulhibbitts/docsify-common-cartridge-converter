# Module 3 - Select and use effective online formative and summative assessment strategies.

**The course outcome is to:**

*Select and use effective online formative and summative assessment strategies to determine student success in achieving the expected learning objectives*

The purpose of this module is to continue using the Understanding by Design framework and identify the tasks or assessments that students will need to complete to demonstrate evidence of understanding and learning and align them with their module learning objectives.

> [!TIP]
> **Learning Objectives**
> 1. Using your course outcomes and module learning objectives, examine and select appropriate formative and summative assessments that students will need to complete to demonstrate evidence of understanding and learning.
> 2. Ensure that  tasks and assessments are aligned with course outcomes and module learning objectives.
