# Module 3 - Final Project

In this final assignment you're going to bring together everything that you've learned over the course of this class - motion tweens, frame by frame animation, using graphic symbols and movie clips, and adding sound to an animation. **You may work with one partner for this project.**

The topic of this final project is up to you (and possibly your partner). However it **MUST** include the following:

1. Be **30 seconds in length** (to fit the length of a standard background music clip).
2. Include **background music** (located at M:/[INGRAM]/[INGRAM - Resources]/_Production Music... or find your own).
3. Include some sort of **snippet of dialogue** (use a soundbite from the Resources folder or find your own). [Movie Sound Clips!](http://www.moviesoundclips.net/) is a good place to start.
4. Have at least **2 new recognizable and developed characters**.
5. Well **detailed background**.
6. At least **2 different scenes/locations**.
7. **Moving objects** - you can use tweens, frame by frame, or graphic symbols to make the movement - your choice.
8. A **title and a credit section** that is no longer than 8 seconds in length in total (note that this can be part of the entire 30 second animation or it can be added onto a 30 second animation).

## How to Submit the Assignment

1. Create a folder in M (Media):\[INGRAM]\2020\2D Animation\Your Name\A8
2. Save the edited animation in .fla and .swf format into this folder.
3. Put a comment in Canvas that you've completed the assignment. If more than one person worked on it, mention both student names in the comment.
