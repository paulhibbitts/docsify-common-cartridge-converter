Welcome to Digital Animation! In this introductory course we'll focus on the basics of animation using Adobe Animate. The emphasis is on project-based, hands-on practice to develop skills and story telling by making a series of short films. If you have a particular skill that you want to explore we can develop a personal project around that interest.

Each module consists of a short video, reading, or demo that explains the concepts needed to successfully complete the projects.
